Pawse

A little friend for your desktop that reminds you to take breaks.

Breaks

Time away from the computer counts as a break, so it won't nag you right after you come back.

Daily rhythm

Behaviour

Playing

  • Click to pet. Double-click for a trick. Drag to pick up and drop.
  • The tray icon has treats, tricks, a break-now button and a way to move to another screen.
  • Clicks go straight through to your apps everywhere except the pet itself.
